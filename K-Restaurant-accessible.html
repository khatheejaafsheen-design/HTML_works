Let's Learn About Making Fried Rice

Ingredients Required

  • Rice - 1 cup
  • Egg - 2
  • Carrot - 1
  • Onion - 1
  • Oil - 2 spoons
  • Salt - As required

Preparation Procedure

  1. Boil the rice and keep it aside.
  2. Heat oil in a pan.
  3. Add chopped onions and carrots.
  4. Add eggs and mix well.
  5. Add the cooked rice.
  6. Add salt and pepper.
  7. Mix everything properly.
  8. Serve hot.

Cooking Information

Preparation Time
10 minutes
Cooking Time
15 minutes
Servings
2 people
